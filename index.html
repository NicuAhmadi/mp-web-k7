The neighborhood

One of Apple Valley's most settled neighborhoods.

Desert Knolls has quiet streets, mature trees, and St. Mary Medical Center a mile up the road — a quick commute for the nurses, techs, and physicians who work there. Desert Mountain Plaza is a third of a mile away. Apple Valley Center — grocery, restaurants, and everyday shopping — sits about half a mile up the road.

Victorville Amtrak is two minutes by car for trips down the hill. Highway 18 puts Big Bear, Lake Arrowhead, and the rest of the mountains inside an hour.
